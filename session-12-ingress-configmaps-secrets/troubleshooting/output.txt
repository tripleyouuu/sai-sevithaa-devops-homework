=== Reproducing the trailing-newline Secret bug ===

$ echo "mypassword" | base64
bXlwYXNzd29yZAo=

$ echo "mypassword" | base64 | base64 -d | xxd | tail -2
00000000: 6d79 7061 7373 776f 7264 0a              mypassword.

--- BUG: trailing 0a (newline) byte included, password is 'mypassword
' not 'mypassword' ---

$ echo -n "mypassword" | base64
bXlwYXNzd29yZA==

$ echo -n "mypassword" | base64 | base64 -d | xxd | tail -2
00000000: 6d79 7061 7373 776f 7264                 mypassword

--- FIX: -n suppresses the newline, password is exactly 'mypassword' (10 bytes, no 0a) ---
